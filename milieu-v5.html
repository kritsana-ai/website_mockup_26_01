🏢 About Milieu

Data intelligence
for a changing Asia

Founded in 2016, Milieu has grown from a Singapore startup into Southeast Asia's leading data intelligence company — serving Fortune 500s, governments, and startups alike.

Our story

Built for Asia, from Asia

Milieu was founded with a simple belief: that Southeast Asia deserved research infrastructure as sophisticated as any market in the world. We built it from scratch — a proprietary panel, a survey platform, and an analytics engine designed for the complexity and diversity of the region.

Today we serve hundreds of clients across 50+ markets, with offices in Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok, and beyond.

🏢
Timeline
How we got here
2016
Milieu Insight founded in Singapore with a mission to democratise market research in Southeast Asia.
2018
Launched Milieu Survey app — grew panel to 500K respondents across 6 SEA markets in 18 months.
2020
Pivoted to COVID-19 tracking studies; delivered 50+ rapid-response reports to governments and NGOs.
2021
Launched Canvas survey platform. Expanded to Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok offices.
2023
Portraits launched — deepest consumer profiling database in SEA. Panel reaches 2M+ members.
2024
Expanded to USA. Recognised as Best Research Technology Platform at ESOMAR Congress.
